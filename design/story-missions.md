# Story, Missions & Survivors

## Decided
- End goal: reach the Core and shut down the AI.
- Survivor camps in each ring: trader, crafting bench, med station, mission-givers.
- Main story chain pushes inward; side missions (rescues, sabotage, supply runs, escorts) reward gear, scrap, and camp upgrades.
- Rescued survivors return to camp and improve it (better stock, new recipes) — rescuing always matters.

## Decided (from playtesting)
- **Opening:** you start with nothing — no weapon, no mission. The first beats are: find the METAL PIPE (glowing item indicator) → fight the Scrapper with melee → loot its wreck for scrap → meet the survivor in the shack → mission: 5 scrap → reward: his old pistol (6 rounds) → he opens up trading (food, ammo, knife).
- The survivor's dialogue tells you he trades if you bring more scrap — trading is introduced through him, not a menu tutorial.
- **The survivor also hands over the YARD GATE KEY** with the pistol — the gate leads out of the junkyard into the Sprawl (Ring 4). The key sits in the ITEMS inventory until the gate exists (next world-building phase hooks onto this).

## THE MAIN STORY IS WRITTEN — see [[main-story]] (design/main-story.md)
THE CENTRAL TWIST: **the amnesiac hero IS Elias Vann**, the man behind the
Correction — erased and discarded by his own uploaded copy after he tried to
undo it. The journey is him discovering his own crime (reveal locked to Q8,
breadcrumbs only before). Ending: the Sunset Command needs his living body,
entering it consumes the creator-lock that protected him, and the system kills
him by reflex as he confirms. Recurring characters: Marek, Ada (only one who
learns the truth). 11-quest chain Q1–Q11.
