CORE SHUTDOWN — City Atlas

The whole city on one board: six rings, every district, every camp, every quest site, every boss. This is a living blueprint — tell Claude what to move, rename, add or delete, and this page updates.

Ring 5 · The Fringe — where you wake up

Collapsed suburbs, rust, overgrowth. The AI barely bothers patrolling here — only Scrappers and stray drones. Palette: rust, amber, dead green.

PlaceTypeWhat it is
JunkyardSTART · Q1 · campMarek's scrap yard (the current playable slice). Pipe, pistol, gate key. Optional depth: THE COMPACTOR — a crusher machine in the yard's deep stacks, first optional mini-boss.
Motorway M7spine roadChoked eight-lane highway running junkyard → Core. The main quest's north road; every ring crosses it.
Candlelightcamp · Q2Burned-out church lit with scavenged neon. First real camp: trader, beds, mission-givers, rumors of the Longest Night.
AldergroveareaOvergrown suburb maze — houses to loot, weak patrols, good tutorial-stealth terrain.
Drone Crash SiteQ3News drone downed mid-broadcast on the Longest Night. Guarded wreck; holds the data-slate with AUTH: E.VANN.
Field 12areaDead airstrip, wide-open sightlines — dangerous crossing, skeleton of a cargo plane to loot.
NorthreachreserveFar northern suburbs. Intentionally empty for future side content.

Ring 4 · The Sprawl — dense ruins

Low-rise ruins, abandoned malls, first drone flocks and turrets. Palette: ochre grey, dust, faded signage.

PlaceTypeWhat it is
Station 9camp · Q4·Q5Survivor camp inside a dead metro station on M7. Ada lives here. Decoding of the slate happens here (Q5 reveal of Vann's name + SUNSET).
Flooded WorkshopQ4Ada's old workshop, knee-deep in river water, drone-patrolled. Retrieve her decryption rig.
The Gallerymini-bossRuined shopping mall, glass roof half-fallen. THE SHEPHERD — a herder unit commanding a drone flock. Optional; drops the first mid-quality tech parts.
DockyardsareaCranes and rusted barges on the Grey Run. Scav territory — human dangers as well as machines (future content hook).
The SinkhazardCollapsed crater spanning into Ring 3 — an unstable shortcut past the Belt's checkpoints. High loot, falling terrain, no patrols (nothing wants to be here).

Ring 3 · The Belt — the factories

Industry still running at full output — the machines build their own reinforcements here. Alarms summon response squads. Palette: iron grey, furnace orange.

PlaceTypeWhat it is
Assembly Plant 03BOSS · Q6·Q7The flagship robot factory; Vann's private lab is hidden inside (hardware key, his logs). Killing the line wakes THE FOREMAN — first mandatory boss. After it falls, WARDEN speaks.
The Blastworksmini-bossFoundry district, rivers of slag. THE SMELTER — a crucible-hauler gone wrong. Optional; guards a cache of mid-quality tech.
Freight YardsareaRail yards feeding the plants — moving trains as hazards, boxcar loot, good ambush terrain for barrel play.
Cinder RowoutpostA toehold camp in a workers' terrace row — last safe bed before the Grid. Small trader, repair bench.
Power ExchangeareaSubstation farm in the NW Belt — future side quest: brown-out the Grid's defenses.

Ring 2 · The Grid — corporate neon

High-rise corporate zone under full surveillance: cameras, elite hunters, shielded units. Stealth matters most here. Palette: dark blue-grey, neon signage.

PlaceTypeWhat it is
CityGrid TowerQ8·Q9WARDEN's old civic headquarters. The Archive (the reveal: his face, his voice, the night it turned on him) and The Offer. Biometric doors that open for exactly one person.
Neon Plazamini-bossThe Grid's glittering central square on M7. After Q9, THE AUDITOR — the elite hunter captain leading the kill-teams — stalks it.
Coreward Bridgemini-bossThe only eastern crossing of the Grey Run toward the center. Held by the TWIN WATCHERS — two shielded units that fight as one.
Monorail LooptransitElevated ring line circling the Core District — riding a dead train past checkpoints is a planned traversal set piece.
The Attichidden cellA survivor listening post in a tower's upper floors. No trade — intel, a bed, and a view of the wall. Ada's contact.

Ring 1 · Core District — the perfect city

Pristine, white, silent, fully machine-run — the finished Correction. Walled. Palette: pale steel, sterile light.

PlaceTypeWhat it is
South WallQ10The rally: every allied camp stages the diversion here while the traveller goes under — the maintenance tunnels run from the wall to the Core.
The SentinelGUARDIANHolds the bridge road (eastern approach). Siege-class walker.
The ChoristerGUARDIANHolds the broadcast spire (northern approach) — WARDEN's voice comes from here; killing it silences the city's speakers.
The SurgeonGUARDIANHolds the medical block (western approach) — repairs other machines mid-fight.

Only ONE Guardian must be fought — whichever approach the player picks (tunnels bypass the wall but surface inside a Guardian's district). The other two remain as the district's roaming apex threats.
