Design document · build plan · Ring 5

The Fringe Wedge

The detailed build for the part of the outer ring we're actually making: four connected areas holding the whole Fringe story. Everything here is changeable — tell Claude what to move, cut or add.

Shape of the whole thing

Not one big map: four hand-authored areas linked at chokepoints, so each can be tuned, lit and populated properly. Total walkable ground ≈ 5× the junkyard. The loop matters — after Candlelight you don't retrace the same road, you come round the north side.

Area 1 · The Approach

Small and deliberately naked. The junkyard taught fighting with walls everywhere; this teaches that outside, patrols see you first.

  1. The gate opens. A service road runs east between collapsed retaining walls; the sky is bigger, the ambience thinner, the wind louder.
  2. The convoy. Six burnt vehicles nose-to-tail — the night everyone tried to drive out at once. Cover, loot, and the first environmental story beat.
  3. First open fight. A Scrapper pair patrolling the road, plus the ring's new rust drone circling above them: you cannot break line of sight by hiding behind one car.
  4. The junction sign. A leaning motorway sign: ALDERGROVE ←, FIELD 12 →, CITY ↑. The player chooses; the game doesn't.

Area 2 · Aldergrove & Candlelight

The meat of the ring, and the first proper settlement. Streets of gutted houses, gardens gone feral, washing still on lines. Enterable interiors are the new toy here.

Area 3 · Field 12 & the crash

A dead airstrip: enormous, flat, exposed. Crossing it is the ring's set piece — long sightlines, patrol drones with real vision cones, and almost nowhere to hide but wreckage.

  1. The runway. Cracked concrete, a skeleton cargo plane, wind-blown ash. Crouch-crossing between wrecks actually matters here.
  2. The crash site. The news drone lies mid-runway, still half-powered, its rotor turning in the wind. Machines guard it — they've been standing there for years.
  3. The recording (Q3). Pull the data-slate: the broadcast shows the machines turning in unison, signed AUTH: E.VANN. First thought-bubble breadcrumb: "...why does that name hurt?"
  4. Optional: the hangar nest. A drone roost in Hangar 3 — a mini-boss-flavoured fight for a forge material and the ring's best loot.

Area 4 · The Underpass

Transitional, short, tense. M7 dives under the collapsed viaduct; the machines have a checkpoint here — the first sign of an organised AI rather than scavenger leftovers.

  1. Under the ridge. Concrete overhead, shafts of light through the cracks, the ambience going metallic and echoing.
  2. The checkpoint. Scanner arch, floodlights, two Scrappers and a mounted sentry — designed to be beatable but to feel like a border.
  3. The threshold. Past it, the road climbs and the Sprawl skyline appears: taller ruins, drone traffic, a wall of city. TO BE CONTINUED lands here until Ring 4 is built.

New enemies · Ring 5 squad roles

Open ground needs threats the yard never had — otherwise the Fringe plays exactly like the junkyard.

RUST DRONE · flyer

Hovers, strafes, pot-shots. Ignores ground cover and crushed junk, so it forces you to move rather than turtle. Weak (2 pistol hits) but relentless — the anti-camping tool.

JUNK-SLINGER · archer

Keeps distance and lobs scrap in an arc that lands where you were. Punishes standing still, rewards closing the gap with melee.

SENTRY POST · turret

Stationary, at the checkpoint only. Area denial that teaches "break the line of sight" before the Sprawl makes it routine.

Build order · each step playable

  1. Area system + The Approach. Walk out the gate into new ground and back again. Proves transitions, per-area save, and the outside ambience.
  2. Block library + Aldergrove streets. The maze, enterable houses, looting. No camp yet.
  3. Candlelight + Q2 + the camp stack. Trader, workbench, respawn moves here.
  4. Field 12 + Q3 + new enemies. The drone crash, the recording, the first story breadcrumb; rust drones and junk-slingers ship with it.
  5. The Underpass + checkpoint. The Ring-4 threshold and the hook for the Sprawl.
