# World & Map

## Decided
- One huge city in concentric rings: Fringe → Sprawl → Industrial Belt → Grid → Core District → Core.
- **Fully open, no gates or walls** — smooth blending between rings, driven per-tile by distance from the Core. The danger is the gate: walk too deep under-geared and you die.
- Map assembled from **small reusable blocks** (street corner, scrap yard, ruined store...) — every edit stays local, never rebuild the whole map.
- Key locations (camps, boss districts) hand-placed; filler stitched from blocks.
- Build starts with a tiny slice of the Fringe and grows outward phase by phase.

## Decided (from playtesting)
- **Minimap:** a small square minimap on screen so you can see what's around you.
- **Enterable buildings** exist from the very first area — small interiors you can walk into.
- The first building holds the **first NPC**.

## Open questions
- **City shape:** perfect circle of rings, or irregular (a river, a collapsed sector, a no-man's-land corridor cutting through)? *(my lean: irregular — more memorable navigation)*
- **Landmarks:** what big silhouettes define the skyline? (the Core tower is one — what else?)
- **Fast travel** between discovered camps: yes/no? *(my lean: yes, camps only)*
- **Underground layer** (metro tunnels, sewers) as risky shortcuts between rings?
- Map screen style (full map later): hand-drawn survivor-style, or clean digital?
- Day/night cycle, weather (rain, ash)?

## Style notes
- Palette journey: rust/amber/overgrowth (outside) → grey industry → cold neon blue/white (Core).
